5.54 MODULE GROUP PURPOSE
The Doctor, Appointment, and Queue Management modules shall collectively manage the administrative workflow through which a patient is scheduled for consultation, arrives at the hospital, checks in, enters an operational queue, and becomes available to the appropriate doctor.
The three modules shall maintain clear business ownership.
DOC │ ▼WHO CAN PROVIDE THE CLINICAL SERVICEAND WHEN ARE THEY AVAILABLE?APT │ ▼WHEN IS THE PATIENT SCHEDULEDTO RECEIVE THE SERVICE?QUE │ ▼WHO IS CURRENTLY WAITINGAND WHO SHOULD BE CALLED NEXT?
These modules shall not own the complete clinical consultation.
Clinical documentation shall be managed by the appropriate clinical modules.
The complete boundary is:
DOCDoctor and Clinical Provider Context              │              ▼APTScheduled Consultation              │              ▼QUEOperational Waiting Flow              │              ▼OPD / EMRClinical Encounter

5.55 END-TO-END PATIENT CONSULTATION ENTRY FLOW
PATIENT   │   ▼SEARCH / IDENTIFY PATIENT   │   ▼SELECT DEPARTMENT / SERVICE   │   ▼SELECT DOCTOR   │   ▼CHECK DOCTOR AVAILABILITY   │   ▼SELECT DATE AND SLOT   │   ▼VALIDATE APPOINTMENT   │   ▼CREATE APPOINTMENT   │   ▼PATIENT ARRIVES   │   ▼CHECK-IN   │   ▼QUEUE ENTRY   │   ▼TOKEN / QUEUE POSITION   │   ▼DOCTOR WORKLIST   │   ▼PATIENT CALLED   │   ▼CLINICAL ENCOUNTER
This flow represents a major operational pathway within CuraSphere.

5.66 APPOINTMENT CREATION
CS-APT-FR-002 — Create Appointment
Requirement Name
Create Appointment
Description
The system shall allow an authorized user to schedule an eligible patient with an eligible doctor for an available appointment time.
Priority
Must Have
Preconditions
The user is authenticated.
The user has appointment creation permission.
The patient exists.
The doctor exists.
The selected scheduling context is valid.
Trigger
The user confirms an appointment booking request.
Main Flow
1. User searches and selects the patient.2. User selects department or consultation context.3. User selects a doctor.4. User selects an available date.5. CuraSphere retrieves candidate appointment slots.6. User selects a candidate slot.7. User submits the appointment request.8. CuraSphere validates patient eligibility.9. CuraSphere validates doctor operational eligibility.10. CuraSphere validates effective doctor availability.11. CuraSphere validates doctor scheduling conflicts.12. CuraSphere validates applicable patient conflicts.13. CuraSphere validates the appointment state and business rules.14. CuraSphere creates the appointment.15. CuraSphere generates the appointment identifier.16. CuraSphere records the appointment event.17. CuraSphere confirms the appointment.
Exception Flow A — Slot Already Occupied
If another appointment occupies the requested slot before final creation:
The system shall reject the conflicting booking.
The system shall not create a duplicate conflicting appointment.
The user shall be informed that the selected time is no longer available.
Updated appointment availability should be provided.
Exception Flow B — Doctor Becomes Unavailable
If the doctor is no longer eligible for the requested time:
The system shall reject the booking.
The appointment shall not be created as confirmed.
Exception Flow C — Invalid Patient
The system shall reject the appointment when the patient is not eligible for the requested workflow.
Business Rules
CS-APT-BR-001
An appointment shall reference an identifiable patient.
CS-APT-BR-002
An appointment shall reference an eligible doctor or approved service scheduling resource.
CS-APT-BR-003
Appointment creation shall perform final server-side schedule validation.
CS-APT-BR-004
Conflicting doctor appointments shall be prevented according to the approved scheduling model.
CS-APT-BR-005
Appointment creation shall be auditable.
Outputs
appointment;
appointment identifier;
appointment status;
confirmation information.
Acceptance Criteria
AC-001
Given an eligible patient, available doctor, and available appointment slot, an authorized user shall be able to create an appointment.
AC-002
Two conflicting confirmed appointments shall not occupy the same exclusive doctor time slot.
AC-003
If the slot becomes occupied before final booking, the later conflicting request shall be rejected.
AC-004
An unauthorized user shall not create an appointment through the protected operation.

5.79 QUE — QUEUE MANAGEMENT
5.79.1 Module Purpose
The Queue Management module shall manage the operational order and state of patients waiting for eligible hospital services.
The first CuraSphere queue scope shall primarily support outpatient consultation flow.
The module shall support:
queue entry creation;
token generation;
queue position management;
patient waiting state;
patient call workflow;
patient skip workflow;
queue recall;
consultation start handoff;
queue completion;
queue cancellation;
doctor worklist;
queue monitoring.
The Queue module answers:
Who is waiting now, for which service or doctor, and what is their current operational waiting state?

5.79.2 Queue Is Not Appointment Scheduling
APPOINTMENT     │     ▼FUTURE / SCHEDULED TIME RESERVATION
Compared with:
QUEUE   │   ▼CURRENT OPERATIONAL WAITING FLOW
Example:
Patient AAppointment: 09:00Arrives: 08:50Checks In: 08:55Patient BAppointment: 09:30Arrives: 08:40Checks In: 08:45
Queue order shall follow the approved queue policy.
It shall not automatically be assumed that:
First physical arrival always goes first
or:
Appointment time alone always determines order
The queue ordering policy shall be defined according to hospital operations.

5.100 MODULE RISK ANALYSIS
Risk 1 — Double Booking
Problem
Two users book the same doctor time simultaneously.
Receptionist A ───► 10:00Receptionist B ───► 10:00
Impact
Two patients may arrive for one exclusive consultation slot.
Mitigation
final backend validation;
transactional booking design;
PostgreSQL concurrency protection;
applicable database constraints.

Risk 2 — Stale Availability
Problem
React displays a slot as available after another user has already booked it.
Mitigation
Displayed slots are candidate availability.
Final booking eligibility shall be revalidated by the backend.

Risk 3 — Doctor Becomes Unavailable
Problem
A doctor goes on leave while future appointments already exist.
Mitigation
Doctor schedule changes shall identify affected appointments and trigger an operational resolution workflow.

Risk 4 — Duplicate Queue Entry
Problem
A patient is checked in multiple times for the same consultation.
Impact
The doctor may call the same patient multiple times or the queue count may become incorrect.
Mitigation
The system shall prevent invalid duplicate active queue entries.

Risk 5 — Wrong Patient Called
Problem
Queue and patient identity information become inconsistent.
Mitigation
Queue entries shall maintain an identifiable patient relationship and use a consistent patient identity summary.

Risk 6 — Queue Position Stored as Permanent Truth
Problem
The database stores:
Patient A = Position 1Patient B = Position 2Patient C = Position 3
but fails to update correctly as queue conditions change.
Mitigation
Queue position shall be treated as a calculated operational value according to queue rules.

Risk 7 — Queue Module Owns Clinical Data
Problem
Developers begin storing:
DiagnosisPrescriptionClinical Notes
inside the queue record.
Mitigation
The Queue module shall manage waiting workflow only.
Clinical information belongs to OPD and EMR domains.

Risk 8 — React State Used as Scheduling Authority
Problem
The frontend assumes:
selectedSlot.available === true
therefore the appointment must be valid.
Mitigation
The trusted backend shall independently validate the complete appointment request.
