6.14 Availability and Reliability Requirements
6.14.1 Introduction
The Availability and Reliability Requirements define how the CuraSphere Hospital Management System shall remain operational, dependable, and resilient during normal operations, peak workloads, hardware failures, software failures, network disruptions, and planned maintenance.
Hospitals operate continuously, providing patient care 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Therefore, CuraSphere shall minimize service interruptions and recover quickly from failures to support uninterrupted clinical and administrative operations.
Availability focuses on keeping the system accessible, while reliability focuses on ensuring the system performs correctly and consistently.

6.14.2 Objectives
The Availability and Reliability Requirements shall ensure that the system:
Remains operational with minimal downtime.
Continues functioning during component failures where possible.
Protects patient care from service interruptions.
Recovers quickly after failures.
Prevents data loss.
Supports continuous monitoring.
Provides dependable service for hospital operations.
Meets defined recovery objectives.

6.14.14 Acceptance Criteria
The system shall be accepted when:
Target availability levels are achieved during operational testing.
Recovery procedures restore services within defined RTO objectives.
Data loss remains within defined RPO objectives.
Failover mechanisms operate successfully in supported deployment environments.
Monitoring identifies failures promptly.
Critical hospital services remain available during supported failure scenarios.
