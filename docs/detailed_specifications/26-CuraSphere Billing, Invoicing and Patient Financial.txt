Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
CuraSphere — Hospital Management System
Section 5: System Functional Requirements
Part 5.8 — Billing, Invoicing and Patient Financial Management System (BIL) Functional Requirements

5.8.1 Introduction
The Billing, Invoicing and Patient Financial Management System (BIL) is the centralized financial transaction management component of the CuraSphere Hospital Management System.
The module shall manage all patient-related financial activities generated by hospital services, including:
Patient consultations
Emergency services
Laboratory investigations
Radiology procedures
Pharmacy purchases
Inpatient admissions
Room and bed charges
Nursing services
Surgical procedures
Operation theatre services
Medical procedures
Doctor charges
Consumable items
Medical equipment usage
Blood bank services
Ambulance services
Insurance-covered services
Government health programs
Discounts
Refunds
Deposits
Advance payments
Outstanding balances
The Billing System shall automatically collect chargeable service information from other CuraSphere modules and generate a centralized patient financial account.
The objective is to prevent manual billing errors, duplicate charges, missed charges, unauthorized discounts, and financial data manipulation.

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

The Billing System shall act as the financial transaction hub of CuraSphere.

5.8.2 Billing System Objectives
The BIL module shall achieve the following objectives:
Centralize all patient billing activities.
Automatically capture charges from hospital departments.
Generate accurate patient invoices.
Support outpatient and inpatient billing.
Manage patient deposits and advance payments.
Process insurance-covered services.
Manage discounts and financial adjustments.
Support multiple payment methods.
Generate official payment receipts.
Track outstanding patient balances.
Prevent unauthorized financial changes.
Maintain complete financial audit records.
Integrate billing with accounting systems.
Provide real-time patient financial status.
Support hospital revenue monitoring.

5.8.5 Functional Requirement BIL-FR-001
Patient Financial Account Creation
Requirement ID
BIL-FR-001
Requirement Name
Create Patient Financial Account
Description
The system shall automatically create and maintain a financial account for every registered patient requiring chargeable hospital services.
The financial account shall act as the centralized record of all patient financial transactions.
Actors
Billing Officer
Cashier
Receptionist
CuraSphere System
Preconditions
Patient must exist in the Master Patient Index.
Patient must possess a valid Patient ID.
Billing services must be operational.
Trigger
A patient receives or is scheduled for a chargeable hospital service.
Main Flow
Patient Registered
        ↓
Chargeable Service Requested
        ↓
System Checks Financial Account
        ↓
Account Exists?
     /        \
   YES        NO
    │          │
Use Account   Create Account
     \        /
      ↓
Financial Account Activated
The system receives a chargeable service request.
The system identifies the patient using the Patient ID.
The system searches for an existing financial account.
If an active account exists, the system shall associate the service with the account.
If no financial account exists, the system shall create a new account.
The system shall assign a unique Financial Account ID.
The account shall be associated with the patient's Master Patient Index record.
The system shall initialize the account balance.
The system shall record the account creation event.
The account shall become available to authorized billing users.
Alternative Flow
A1 — Existing Active Financial Account
The system shall use the patient's existing financial account.
A2 — Inpatient Encounter
The system may create an encounter-specific financial account for the admission.
A3 — Emergency Unidentified Patient
The system shall create a temporary emergency financial account associated with the temporary patient identity.
Exception Flow
E1 — Invalid Patient ID
The system shall reject financial account creation.
E2 — Duplicate Financial Account
The system shall prevent unauthorized duplicate account creation.
E3 — Billing Service Failure
The system shall log the failure and queue the account creation operation for authorized review.
Postconditions
Patient financial account is available.
Account is associated with the patient.
Financial transactions may be recorded.
Business Rules
BIL-BR-001: Every financial account shall belong to a valid patient or authorized temporary emergency identity.
BIL-BR-002: Financial Account IDs shall be globally unique.
BIL-BR-003: Financial accounts shall not be physically deleted.
BIL-BR-004: Account status changes shall be audited.

5.8.6 Functional Requirement BIL-FR-002
Automatic Service Charge Capture
Requirement ID
BIL-FR-002
Requirement Name
Automatically Capture Hospital Service Charges
Description
The system shall automatically receive and record financial charges generated by integrated CuraSphere hospital modules.
Manual re-entry of service charges should not be required when the originating module is integrated with the Billing System.
Actors
CuraSphere System
Billing Officer
Department Systems
Finance Officer
Preconditions
Patient financial account must exist.
Chargeable service must exist in the Service Charge Master.
Source module must be authorized.
Trigger
A hospital department records a chargeable service as ordered, dispensed, performed, consumed, or completed according to the configured billing rule.
Main Flow
Service Performed
       ↓
Source Module Creates Charge Event
       ↓
Billing Engine Receives Event
       ↓
Validate Patient
       ↓
Validate Encounter
       ↓
Validate Service Code
       ↓
Retrieve Price
       ↓
Calculate Charge
       ↓
Post to Financial Account
       ↓
Update Patient Balance
       ↓
Create Audit Log
A hospital module generates a charge event.
The Billing Engine receives the event.
The system identifies the source module.
The system validates the Patient ID.
The system validates the Encounter ID.
The system validates the Service Code.
The system retrieves the active service price.
The system determines the applicable pricing category.
The system calculates the charge.
The system creates a billing charge transaction.
The charge shall be posted to the patient's financial account.
The outstanding balance shall be recalculated.
The system shall acknowledge successful charge posting to the source module.
An audit record shall be generated.
Charge Event Model
{
    Charge Event ID
    Patient ID
    Encounter ID
    Source Module
    Department ID
    Service Code
    Service Description
    Quantity
    Unit Price
    Total Amount

}
Alternative Flow
A1 — Insurance Patient
The charge shall be evaluated against insurance coverage rules.
A2 — Package Patient
The system shall check whether the service is included in an active healthcare package.
A3 — Government Program
The system shall evaluate government program coverage.
A4 — Zero-Cost Service
The system shall record the service with a zero financial value and applicable reason.
Exception Flow
E1 — Invalid Service Code
The charge event shall be rejected and logged.
E2 — Missing Price
The charge shall enter the Billing Exception Queue.
E3 — Duplicate Charge Event
The system shall detect the duplicate event and prevent duplicate posting.
E4 — Financial Account Not Found
The charge shall remain pending until account resolution.
Business Rules
BIL-BR-005: Every automated charge shall have a source module.
BIL-BR-006: Every charge event shall possess a unique Charge Event ID.
BIL-BR-007: Duplicate charge events shall not create duplicate financial transactions.
BIL-BR-008: The price active at the applicable service pricing time shall be used according to configured policy.
BIL-BR-009: Manual changes to automatically generated charges require authorization.
Audit and Logging

Source Module
Patient ID
Service Code
Original Price
Applied Price
Quantity
Total Charge
Transaction Time
Posting Status
Acceptance Criteria
Integrated modules can automatically submit charges.
Valid charges appear in the correct financial account.
Duplicate events are prevented.
Missing pricing information is detected.
Patient balance is automatically recalculated.
Charge events are fully traceable.
