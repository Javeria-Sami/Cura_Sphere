10. Reports, Dashboards & Business Intelligence (BI)

10.1 Introduction
The Reports, Dashboards & Business Intelligence (BI) Module provides comprehensive analytical, operational, financial, and clinical reporting capabilities within the CuraSphere Hospital Management System.
This module transforms operational data into meaningful insights that support:
Executive decision-making
Clinical governance
Financial management
Regulatory compliance
Operational monitoring
Resource planning
Performance optimization
Quality improvement
Predictive healthcare analytics
The Business Intelligence module shall consolidate data from all integrated modules into a centralized reporting environment.

10.1.1 Objectives
The Reporting & BI Module shall enable healthcare organizations to:
Monitor hospital performance in real time.
Analyze clinical outcomes.
Track financial performance.
Measure operational efficiency.
Generate regulatory reports.
Support accreditation requirements.
Improve patient care quality.
Identify operational bottlenecks.
Assist executive decision-making.
Support data-driven planning.

10.1.15 Performance Requirements
The Reporting Dashboard shall:
Load the initial dashboard within configurable performance targets under normal operating conditions.
Retrieve report metadata without blocking the user interface.
Support asynchronous loading of dashboard widgets.
Cache frequently accessed report definitions where appropriate.
Display loading indicators during data retrieval.
Continue operating when non-critical widgets fail to load.

10.1.16 Accessibility Requirements
The Reporting Dashboard shall:
Support keyboard-only navigation.
Provide screen-reader compatible charts and controls.
Include descriptive labels for widgets and KPIs.
Ensure sufficient color contrast for charts and indicators.
Support text scaling up to 200%.
Comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA requirements.

10.1.17 Security Requirements
The Reporting & BI Dashboard shall:
Enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
Restrict report visibility based on user permissions.
Mask sensitive patient and financial information where required.
Log report generation, export, scheduling, and sharing activities.
Protect report data during transmission using HTTPS/TLS.
Require authorization before accessing confidential reports.

10.1.18 Acceptance Criteria
The Reporting & Business Intelligence Overview shall be accepted when:
Users can access reports according to their assigned roles.
Report categories are clearly organized and searchable.
Dashboard widgets display accurate and timely information.
Favorite, recent, and scheduled reports function correctly.
Personalization settings persist across user sessions.
Performance, accessibility, security, and auditing requirements are fully satisfied.

10.2 Enterprise Reports Dashboard

10.2.1 Introduction
The Enterprise Reports Dashboard serves as the centralized analytical workspace for executives, hospital administrators, department managers, and operational teams. It consolidates Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), operational metrics, financial summaries, and clinical outcomes into a unified interface for strategic and tactical decision-making.
The dashboard shall present real-time, near real-time, and historical data using configurable visualizations and interactive components.

10.2.2 Objectives
The Enterprise Reports Dashboard shall enable users to:
Monitor organizational performance.
Track hospital-wide KPIs.
Identify operational bottlenecks.
Measure departmental efficiency.
Analyze financial trends.
Evaluate patient care quality.
Monitor resource utilization.
Compare historical performance.
Support executive decision-making.
