3. STAKEHOLDER REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Purpose
The purpose of this section is to identify the stakeholders of CuraSphere and formally document their high-level needs, expectations, responsibilities, and system requirements.
A stakeholder is any person, group, department, or organization that:
uses CuraSphere;
receives information from CuraSphere;
provides information to CuraSphere;
manages CuraSphere;
maintains CuraSphere;
is affected by CuraSphere;
makes decisions based on CuraSphere information.
Stakeholder requirements describe what stakeholders need from the system.
They do not yet describe the detailed internal implementation of the software.
The requirements engineering relationship is:
STAKEHOLDER     │     ▼PROBLEM / NEED     │     ▼STAKEHOLDER REQUIREMENT     │     ▼SYSTEM REQUIREMENT     │     ▼FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENT     │     ▼USE CASE     │     ▼USER STORY     │     ▼UI / API / DATA DESIGN     │     ▼IMPLEMENTATION     │     ▼TEST CASE
For example:
Stakeholder:ReceptionistProblem:Patient registration takes too much time.Need:A fast digital patient registration process.Stakeholder Requirement:CS-REC-SR-001System Requirement:The system shall support digital patient registration.Functional Requirement:The system shall allow an authorized receptionistto create a patient record.Use Case:UC-PAT-001 Register PatientUI:Patient Registration FormAPI:Patient registration operationData:Patient administrative recordTest:Verify successful patient registration.
This traceability model shall be maintained throughout CuraSphere development.

3.4 Stakeholder Requirement Priority Model
Each stakeholder requirement shall be assigned a priority.
The MoSCoW model shall be used.
Priority
Meaning
Must Have
Required for system operation or critical business workflow
Should Have

Could Have

Won’t Have Now

PROPOSED    │    ▼UNDER REVIEW    │    ▼APPROVED    │    ▼BASELINED    │    ▼IMPLEMENTED    │    ▼VERIFIED

Approved changes shall follow the requirements change control process.

3.6 Hospital Owner or Governing Authority Requirements
Stakeholder Description
The Hospital Owner or Governing Authority is responsible for the strategic direction and sustainability of the healthcare organization.
Primary Problems
limited visibility into hospital performance;
delayed management reports;
weak financial transparency;
difficulty measuring operational efficiency;
limited understanding of resource utilization.
Primary Needs
The stakeholder needs reliable, summarized, and authorized hospital information for strategic decision-making.

CS-OWN-SR-001 — Hospital Performance Visibility
Priority: Must Have
The hospital owner shall be able to access authorized high-level hospital performance information.
The information should include summarized indicators relating to:
patient activity;
hospital service utilization;
admissions;
hospital occupancy;
financial activity;
operational trends.

CS-OWN-SR-002 — Management Reporting
Priority: Must Have
The hospital owner shall be able to access approved management reports without requiring manual report compilation from multiple hospital departments.

CS-OWN-SR-003 — Financial Visibility
Priority: Must Have
The hospital owner shall be able to review authorized financial summaries generated from recorded hospital financial activities.

CS-OWN-SR-004 — Operational Trend Analysis
Priority: Should Have
The hospital owner should be able to review operational trends across defined periods.

CS-OWN-SR-005 — Controlled Information Access
Priority: Must Have
Strategic access shall not automatically provide permission to modify clinical information.

Primary Problems
long registration time;
repeated information requests;
long waiting periods;
lost medical history;
difficulty obtaining reports;
unclear billing information.

CS-PAT-SR-001 — Unique Patient Record
Priority: Must Have

CS-PAT-SR-002 — Medical Record Continuity
Priority: Must Have
Authorized hospital users shall be able to maintain the continuity of a patient’s recorded medical history.

CS-PAT-SR-003 — Reduced Duplicate Data Collection
Priority: Should Have

CS-PAT-SR-004 — Appointment Information
Priority: Must Have

CS-PAT-SR-005 — Billing Transparency

CS-PAT-SR-006 — Patient Information Privacy
Priority: Must Have

CS-PAT-SR-007 — Patient Portal
Priority: Could Have

patient safety;

security and privacy;
hospital business policy;
clinical workflow requirements;
operational efficiency;
usability;

3.33 Stakeholder Requirement Summary
The stakeholder analysis identifies several core themes.
Theme 1 — Centralized Information
Hospital stakeholders require a shared information platform.
Theme 2 — Controlled Access
Different users require different levels of information access and operational authority.
Theme 3 — Clinical Continuity
Doctors and nurses require accurate patient history and care information.
Theme 4 — Workflow Efficiency
Reception, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy, and billing require organized digital worklists and workflows.
Theme 5 — Accountability
Sensitive actions require traceability and auditability.
Theme 6 — Management Visibility
Hospital leadership requires reliable operational and financial information.
Theme 7 — Technical Sustainability
Developers, QA engineers, IT Support, and DevOps require a maintainable, testable, observable, and deployable system.
