6.18 Backup, Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity Requirements

6.18.1 Introduction
Healthcare organizations operate continuously and cannot afford prolonged service interruptions or data loss. Patient safety, clinical operations, financial transactions, laboratory services, and emergency care depend on the continuous availability and integrity of hospital information systems.
The CuraSphere Hospital Management System shall implement a comprehensive Backup, Disaster Recovery (DR), and Business Continuity (BC) framework to ensure that critical hospital services remain available during planned maintenance, hardware failures, cyber incidents, natural disasters, software failures, power outages, and human errors.
The system shall support enterprise-grade recovery capabilities to minimize operational disruption while protecting patient information and business-critical data.

6.18.2 Purpose
The purpose of this section is to define the backup policies, disaster recovery architecture, recovery objectives, continuity planning, testing procedures, and operational requirements necessary to ensure uninterrupted healthcare services.

6.18.3 Objectives
The Backup, Disaster Recovery, and Business Continuity framework shall:
Protect critical healthcare information.
Minimize downtime.
Minimize data loss.
Ensure rapid recovery after disasters.
Maintain essential hospital operations.
Protect system configurations.
Support regulatory compliance.
Ensure business resilience.
Improve operational reliability.
Protect organizational reputation.

6.18.21 Acceptance Criteria
The Backup and Disaster Recovery solution shall be accepted when:
All scheduled backups complete successfully.
Backup integrity validation passes.
Recovery objectives (RTO and RPO) are achieved during testing.
Disaster recovery drills demonstrate successful restoration of critical services.
Business continuity procedures allow essential hospital functions to continue during major outages.
Backup data remains encrypted and access-controlled throughout its lifecycle.

6.18.23 Section Summary
The Backup, Disaster Recovery, and Business Continuity framework provides a comprehensive strategy for protecting CuraSphere against data loss and operational disruption. Through layered backups, secure offsite replication, disaster recovery planning, validated recovery procedures, and continuity measures, the system supports resilient healthcare operations and safeguards critical patient and organizational information.
