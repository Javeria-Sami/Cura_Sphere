16.1 Performance Requirements

16.1.1 Introduction
The Performance Requirements define the measurable operational characteristics of the CuraSphere Hospital Management System (HMS) to ensure that it delivers fast, reliable, and efficient services under normal and peak operating conditions.
Performance is a critical quality attribute in healthcare systems because delays in accessing patient information, processing laboratory results, scheduling appointments, or generating prescriptions may directly impact patient care and hospital operations.
The objective of this section is to establish performance benchmarks that support:
Rapid system response
High throughput
Efficient resource utilization
Scalability
Low latency
Continuous availability
Excellent user experience

16.1.2 Performance Objectives
The CuraSphere system shall achieve the following objectives:
Provide near real-time access to patient records.
Minimize response time for clinical workflows.
Maintain acceptable performance during peak hospital hours.
Support concurrent access by multiple users.
Ensure efficient processing of laboratory and radiology data.
Prevent system bottlenecks during high workloads.
Support future organizational growth without significant performance degradation.

As CuraSphere supports critical healthcare operations, system reliability is essential to ensure uninterrupted patient care, accurate clinical documentation, secure financial transactions, and dependable communication across hospital departments.
The objectives of this section are to ensure:
Continuous system operation
Accurate transaction processing
Reliable data storage
Consistent application behavior
Fault tolerance
Rapid recovery from failures
Protection against data loss

Operate continuously during normal hospital operations.
Maintain data consistency across all modules.
Recover gracefully from unexpected failures.
Prevent duplicate or incomplete transactions.
Preserve patient information during hardware or software failures.
Minimize service interruptions.
Ensure reliable communication with integrated systems and medical devices.

The objectives of this section are to:
Protect patient confidentiality.
Maintain data integrity.
Ensure service availability.
Prevent unauthorized access.
Detect and respond to security incidents.
Support regulatory compliance.
Protect healthcare infrastructure from cyber threats.

16.5.2 Security Objectives
The CuraSphere system shall achieve the following security objectives:
Ensure confidentiality of patient and organizational data.
Prevent unauthorized modification of healthcare records.
Maintain continuous availability of critical services.
Authenticate every user before granting access.
Enforce least-privilege access control.
Detect suspicious activities in real time.
Maintain complete audit trails.
Support secure integration with external healthcare systems.
