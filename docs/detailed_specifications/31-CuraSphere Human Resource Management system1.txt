Part 5.11 - Human Resource Management System (HRM)

5.11.1 Introduction
The Human Resource Management System (HRM) is responsible for managing the complete employee lifecycle within the CuraSphere Hospital Management System. It centralizes all human resource operations, including recruitment, employee records, credentialing, attendance, scheduling, payroll integration, leave management, performance evaluation, training, and employee separation.
A hospital relies heavily on qualified medical and non-medical staff. Therefore, the HRM module ensures that every employee is properly recruited, verified, scheduled, monitored, evaluated, and compensated while complying with healthcare regulations and labor laws.

5.11.5 Functional Requirement HRM-FR-001
Employee Master Management

Requirement ID
HRM-FR-001
Requirement Name
Employee Master Record Management

Description
The system shall maintain a centralized employee database containing all hospital personnel.
The Employee Master shall serve as the primary source of employee information across all CuraSphere modules.

Actors
HR Officer
HR Manager
System Administrator

Preconditions
User must have HR management permissions.

Trigger
A new employee is hired or an existing employee's information is updated.

Main Workflow
Employee Hired
      │
      ▼
Create Employee Record
      │
      ▼
Assign Employee ID
      │
      ▼
Validate Information
      │
      ▼
Save Record
      │
      ▼
Audit Log Created

Employee Information
The Employee Master shall maintain:
Employee ID
National ID / Passport
Full Name
Gender
Date of Birth
Contact Number
Email Address
Residential Address
Emergency Contact
Department
Designation
Employment Type
Date of Joining
Employment Status
Reporting Manager
Qualifications
Professional License Number (where applicable)

Business Rules
HRM-BR-001
Each employee shall have a unique Employee ID.
HRM-BR-002
Employee records shall not be permanently deleted.
HRM-BR-003
Only authorized HR personnel may modify employee information.

Acceptance Criteria
Employee records created successfully.
Duplicate Employee IDs prevented.
Audit logs generated.
