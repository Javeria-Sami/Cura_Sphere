Why this stack?
This combination is:
Modern and widely used in industry.
Scalable for small clinics or large hospitals.
Strongly typed with TypeScript and C#.
Secure with JWT and ASP.NET Identity.
Maintainable through reusable UI components and clean architecture.
Well suited for dashboards, reporting, and data-heavy healthcare applications.
