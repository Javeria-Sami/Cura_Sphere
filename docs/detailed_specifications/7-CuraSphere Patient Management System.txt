5.24 PAT — PATIENT MANAGEMENT
5.24.1 Module Purpose
The Patient Management module shall manage the administrative identity of patients receiving healthcare services through CuraSphere.
The PAT module shall provide the controlled patient identity foundation required by clinical, diagnostic, pharmacy, admission, billing, and reporting workflows.
The module shall support:
patient registration;
patient identification;
Medical Record Number management;
patient search;
patient demographic management;
patient contact information;
address information;
emergency contacts;
patient status;
duplicate patient detection;
patient identity review;
patient record merge governance;
patient administrative history.
The Patient Management module shall primarily manage the administrative identity of a patient.
It shall not become the owner of the complete clinical medical record.
The fundamental boundary is:
PAT MODULE    │    ▼WHO IS THE PATIENT?EMR MODULE    │    ▼WHAT CLINICAL CARE HAS THE PATIENT RECEIVED?
For example:
PAT OWNSPatient IdentifierMRNNameDate of BirthAdministrative Sex / Gender DataContact InformationAddressEmergency ContactPatient Status
Compared with:
EMR OWNSClinical EncountersMedical HistoryClinical NotesAllergiesDiagnosesClinical Summaries
This separation shall be maintained throughout CuraSphere design.

5.24.2 Why Patient Identity Is a Critical Domain
A patient record is referenced by multiple CuraSphere modules.
                            PATIENT                               │         ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐         │                     │                     │         ▼                     ▼                     ▼     APPOINTMENT              OPD                   IPD         │                     │                     │         └─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┘                               │                               ▼                              EMR                               │             ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐             │                 │                 │             ▼                 ▼                 ▼            LAB               RAD               PRE                                                   │                                                   ▼                                                  PHAPATIENT ──────────────────────► BILPATIENT ──────────────────────► PAYPATIENT ──────────────────────► INS
An incorrect patient identity may create serious downstream problems.
Example:
WRONG PATIENT SELECTED         │         ▼WRONG CLINICAL ENCOUNTER         │         ▼WRONG LAB REQUEST         │         ▼RESULT ATTACHED TO WRONG RECORD         │         ▼CLINICAL DECISION RISK
Another example:
DUPLICATE PATIENT CREATEDPatient Record AMRN: MRN-10021Patient Record BMRN: MRN-20541Both represent the same real person.
This can divide the patient’s history.
PATIENT HISTORY      │      ├────► RECORD A      │        ├── OPD Visit      │        └── Allergy      │      └────► RECORD B               ├── Lab Result               └── Prescription
Therefore, patient registration, search, duplicate detection, and identity correction shall be treated as controlled business workflows.

5.25 PATIENT SEARCH REQUIREMENTS
CS-PAT-FR-001 — Search Patient
Requirement Name
Search Patient
Module
PAT — Patient Management
Requirement Type
Functional Requirement
Description
The system shall allow an authorized user to search for an existing patient before creating a new patient record or performing a patient-related workflow.
Source
CS-REC-SR-001 — Patient Search
Primary Actor
Receptionist
Secondary Actors
Doctor
Nurse
Billing Officer
Authorized Hospital User
Priority
Must Have
Preconditions
The user is authenticated.
The user has patient search permission.
The Patient Management module is available.
Trigger
The user initiates a patient search.
Main Requirement
The system shall accept approved patient search criteria and return matching patient records that the requesting user is authorized to view.
Main Flow
1. User opens patient search.2. CuraSphere presents the patient search interface.3. User enters one or more search criteria.4. CuraSphere validates the search criteria.5. CuraSphere applies applicable search rules.6. CuraSphere searches patient records.7. CuraSphere applies authorization and information visibility rules.8. CuraSphere returns matching patient records.9. User reviews the results.10. User selects an eligible patient record.
Search Criteria May Include
Medical Record Number;
patient name;
phone number;
national identifier where configured;
date of birth;
patient internal identifier where appropriate.
Alternate Flow A — No Patient Found
If no matching patient is found:
The system shall display a controlled no-result state.
The system may allow an authorized user to initiate patient registration.
The search criteria may be reused where appropriate.
Alternate Flow B — Multiple Patients Found
If multiple patient records match:
The system shall display permitted identifying information.
The user shall review the results.
The system shall not automatically select a patient solely because the first result matched.
Exception Flow — Invalid Search
If the search request is invalid:
The system shall reject the invalid search request.
The system shall provide appropriate validation feedback.
Business Rules
CS-PAT-BR-001
Authorized users should search for an existing patient before registering a new patient.
CS-PAT-BR-002
Search results shall only expose patient information permitted for the requesting user.
CS-PAT-BR-003
The system shall not assume that two patients are the same solely because their names match.
Inputs
One or more approved patient search criteria.
Outputs
A list of permitted patient search results.
Postconditions
No patient information is modified by a normal search operation.
Security Considerations
Patient search is a protected operation.
The ability to search patients shall not automatically provide unrestricted access to the patient’s clinical record.
Acceptance Criteria
AC-001
Given an authorized user and a valid existing MRN, when the user searches using the MRN, then the matching permitted patient record shall be returned.
AC-002
Given a valid search with no matching patient, when the search completes, then the system shall show a no-result state.
AC-003
Given an unauthorized user, when the user attempts patient search, then the protected operation shall be denied.
AC-004
Given multiple matching patients, the system shall return permitted matching results without automatically treating them as the same person.
Dependencies
IAM — Identity and Access Management
Traceability

Main Flow Steps
The user opens the patient registration interface.
CuraSphere presents the approved registration form.
The user enters patient administrative information.
The user submits the registration request.
CuraSphere validates required information.
CuraSphere normalizes applicable registration data.
CuraSphere evaluates probable duplicate patient records.
If no blocking condition exists, CuraSphere creates a unique patient internal identifier.
CuraSphere generates or assigns the Medical Record Number according to the MRN policy.
CuraSphere stores the patient administrative record.
CuraSphere records the registration event.
CuraSphere confirms successful patient registration.
Alternate Flow A — Probable Duplicate Found
If probable duplicate records are identified:
CuraSphere shall display an appropriate duplicate warning.
CuraSphere shall show permitted identity comparison information.
The user shall review the possible matches.
The user may select an existing patient where appropriate.
If permitted by business rules, the user may continue new patient registration after explicit confirmation.
The system shall record applicable duplicate warning information.
Alternate Flow B — Temporary Patient
If the patient cannot be fully identified and the workflow permits temporary registration:
The user initiates temporary patient registration.
The system collects the minimum required temporary information.
The system creates a temporary patient identity.
The temporary status is clearly identifiable.
The system allows the approved hospital workflow to continue.
Exception Flow A — Missing Required Data
The system shall reject normal registration when required patient information is missing.
Exception Flow B — Invalid Data
The system shall reject invalid patient information according to validation rules.
Exception Flow C — MRN Generation Failure
If a valid MRN cannot be generated or assigned:
Patient registration shall not be reported as successfully completed.
The system shall preserve transactional consistency.
The technical failure shall be handled according to application error rules.
Business Rules
CS-PAT-BR-004
A new patient shall receive a unique internal patient identifier.
CS-PAT-BR-005
A patient shall receive an MRN according to the approved MRN policy.
CS-PAT-BR-006

CS-PAT-BR-007

CS-PAT-BR-008

CS-PAT-BR-009

5.27 PATIENT IDENTIFIER REQUIREMENTS
CS-PAT-FR-003 — Generate Patient Internal Identifier
Description
The system shall assign a unique internal system identifier to every patient record.
Priority
Must Have
Purpose
The internal patient identifier shall provide a stable technical identity for the patient record.
Conceptually:
PATIENT ID    │    ▼SYSTEM IDENTITYMRN    │    ▼BUSINESS / MEDICAL RECORD IDENTIFIER
These concepts shall not be assumed to be identical.
Example
Internal Patient ID:6f3a...technical identifierMRN:CS-MRN-00001245
The exact technical identifier format shall be selected during architecture and data design.
Business Rule
The internal patient identifier shall not be manually reused for another patient record.

CS-PAT-FR-004 — Generate Medical Record Number
Description
The system shall generate or assign a Medical Record Number to an eligible patient according to the approved MRN policy.
Priority
Must Have
Purpose of MRN
The MRN shall provide a hospital-recognizable medical record identifier.
Conceptual MRN Examples
MRN-00000001CS-000012452026-001245
These are examples only.
The final format shall be approved in the Patient Identifier Design Specification.
MRN Requirements
The MRN shall:
be unique within its defined scope;
identify the corresponding patient record;
not be silently reassigned to another patient;
remain traceable where correction or migration is required.
Important Engineering Rule
MRN generation shall not rely only on:
SELECT MAX(MRN) + 1
without a concurrency-safe design.
Example risk:
Receptionist A reads maximum = 100Receptionist B reads maximum = 100A generates 101B generates 101DUPLICATE MRN
The final PostgreSQL design shall provide concurrency-safe identifier generation.
Acceptance Criteria
Two successfully registered patients shall not receive the same MRN within the configured MRN scope.

CS-PAT-FR-006 — Detect Probable Duplicate Patient
Description

Source

Priority
Must Have
Duplicate Detection Inputs May Include
normalized patient name;
date of birth;
phone number;

other approved identity attributes.
Conceptual Model
NEW PATIENT DATA       │       ▼NORMALIZATION       │       ▼DUPLICATE EVALUATION       │ ┌─────┼────────────┐ │     │            │ ▼     ▼            ▼NAME   DOB        CONTACT │     │            │ └─────┼────────────┘       │       ▼POTENTIAL MATCH SCORE / RULE       │   ┌───┴───┐   │       │ MATCH   NO MATCH   │       │   ▼       ▼WARNING  CONTINUE
Important Requirement
The duplicate detection mechanism shall identify probable duplicates.
It shall not automatically merge patient records solely because a similarity rule matched.
Example
NEW REGISTRATIONName:Muhammad AliDOB:2000-04-12Phone:0300XXXX111POSSIBLE EXISTING RECORDName:Muhammad AliDOB:2000-04-12Phone:0300XXXX111
This is a strong possible match.
Compared with:
Name:Muhammad AliDOB:1981-09-21Phone:Different
A name match alone may be insufficient.
Business Rules
CS-PAT-BR-010
Duplicate detection shall use approved matching criteria.
CS-PAT-BR-011
A probable duplicate shall not automatically result in a patient record merge.
CS-PAT-BR-012
Duplicate detection results shall be presented only to authorized users.
Acceptance Criteria
When registration data satisfies an approved probable duplicate rule, the system shall trigger a duplicate warning or review process.

CS-PAT-FR-018 — Merge Duplicate Patient Records
Description
The system should support a controlled process for merging confirmed duplicate patient records.
Priority
Should Have
Risk Classification
High-Risk Data Operation
Important Requirement
Patient record merging shall not be implemented as:
DELETE Patient B
The merge process must consider records referencing both patient identities.
Example:
PATIENT A   │   ├── OPD Encounter   ├── Allergy   └── InvoicePATIENT B   │   ├── Lab Result   ├── Prescription   └── Appointment

PATIENT A ──┐            │            ▼      SURVIVING PATIENT            ▲            │PATIENT B ──┘
The merge workflow must preserve traceability.
Conceptual Merge Flow
CONFIRMED DUPLICATE        │        ▼SELECT SURVIVING RECORD        │        ▼REVIEW IDENTITY DATA        │        ▼REVIEW DEPENDENT REFERENCES        │        ▼VALIDATE MERGE ELIGIBILITY        │        ▼AUTHORIZED CONFIRMATION        │        ▼EXECUTE CONTROLLED MERGE        │        ▼MARK DUPLICATE RECORD AS MERGED        │        ▼PRESERVE MERGE REFERENCE        │        ▼AUDIT MERGE EVENT
Business Rules
CS-PAT-BR-019

CS-PAT-BR-020

CS-PAT-BR-021

CS-PAT-BR-022

CS-PAT-BR-023

CS-PAT-BR-024

5.51 PATIENT MODULE RISK ANALYSIS
Risk 1 — Duplicate Patient Records
Problem
The same real patient may have multiple CuraSphere patient records.
Example
MRN-10021Muhammad AhmadMRN-20441Mohammad Ahmed
Both may represent the same person.
Impact
divided medical history;
duplicate testing;
incorrect clinical decisions;
billing confusion;
inaccurate reports.
Mitigation
search-before-register workflow;
duplicate detection;
duplicate review;
controlled merge process.

Risk 2 — Wrong Patient Selection
Problem
A user selects the wrong patient with a similar name.
Impact
The wrong patient’s record may receive:
consultation information;
laboratory requests;
prescriptions;
billing charges.
Mitigation
The UI architecture shall use a consistent patient identity summary and appropriate identity confirmation patterns.

Risk 3 — MRN Collision
Problem
Two patients receive the same Medical Record Number.
Impact
Patient identity integrity is compromised.
Mitigation
MRN generation shall use a concurrency-safe technical design and database-level uniqueness protection.

Risk 4 — Frontend-Only Validation
Problem
The React application validates patient information, but the API accepts invalid direct requests.
Mitigation
Validation responsibilities shall be applied at appropriate frontend, backend, domain, and database boundaries.

Risk 5 — Uncontrolled Patient Deletion
Problem
Deleting a patient record may break clinical and financial references.
Mitigation
Normal patient workflows shall not provide unrestricted permanent patient deletion.

Risk 6 — Incorrect Patient Merge
Problem
Two different real people may be merged into one record.
Impact
This is a high-risk data integrity and clinical safety problem.
Mitigation
Patient merge shall require:
duplicate review;
enhanced authorization;
explicit surviving record selection;
transaction control;
audit traceability.

Risk 7 — Invented Emergency Patient Information
Problem
Staff may enter false names or dates of birth because the software requires complete permanent patient information.
Mitigation
CuraSphere shall support an approved temporary patient identity workflow.
