Part 5.13 - Notification and Communication Management System (NCM)

5.13.1 Introduction
The Notification and Communication Management System (NCM) is responsible for delivering timely, secure, and reliable communication between the CuraSphere Hospital Management System, patients, healthcare professionals, administrators, and external communication services.
The module provides automated notifications, reminders, alerts, announcements, and messaging to improve patient care, reduce missed appointments, increase operational efficiency, and ensure that critical information reaches the appropriate users.
The Notification System supports both real-time and scheduled communication through multiple delivery channels.
