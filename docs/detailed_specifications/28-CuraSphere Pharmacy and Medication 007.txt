5.9.11 Functional Requirement PHR-FR-007
Controlled Drug (Narcotics) Management

Requirement ID
PHR-FR-007
Requirement Name
Controlled Drug and Narcotics Management

Description
The system shall provide a secure mechanism for managing controlled medicines, narcotics, psychotropic drugs, and other legally regulated pharmaceutical products.
Controlled medicines require stricter inventory control, authorization, dispensing, storage, and audit logging than standard medicines.
The module shall comply with hospital policies and national pharmaceutical regulations.

Actors
Pharmacist
Pharmacy Manager
Authorized Doctor
Inventory Officer
Hospital Administrator
Regulatory Auditor

Preconditions
Medicine exists in Medicine Master.
Medicine is classified as Controlled Drug.
User possesses controlled medicine privileges.

Trigger
A controlled medicine is prescribed, received, dispensed, transferred, adjusted, or destroyed.

Controlled Drug ID

Patient ID
Prescribing Doctor
Dispensing Pharmacist
Quantity Dispensed
Remaining Stock
Batch Number
Expiry Date
Storage Location
Approval Information
Regulatory Reference

Alternative Flow
Emergency medication override may be performed by authorized personnel according to hospital emergency policy.

Exception Flow
Unauthorized user attempts dispensing.
System shall:
Block transaction.
Record security event.
Notify pharmacy supervisor.

Business Rules
PHR-BR-015
Controlled medicines require pharmacist verification.
PHR-BR-016
Controlled drug inventory shall never become negative.
PHR-BR-017
Every controlled drug transaction shall be audited.
PHR-BR-018
Controlled medicines require barcode verification before dispensing.

Acceptance Criteria
✔ Unauthorized dispensing prevented
✔ Controlled drug register maintained
✔ Audit generated
✔ Inventory synchronized

5.9.21 Functional Requirement PHR-FR-017
Pharmacy Non-Functional Requirements
The Pharmacy System shall provide:
Performance
Medicine search shall typically complete within 2 seconds under normal operating conditions.
Availability
Target availability shall be 99.9%, excluding planned maintenance.
Reliability
Medicine dispensing shall use transactional processing to prevent inconsistent inventory or billing records.
Security
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall protect pharmacy operations.
Scalability
The architecture shall support multiple pharmacy locations and future expansion.
Backup
Medication records shall be included in scheduled hospital backup and disaster recovery procedures.
Compliance
The system shall support applicable pharmaceutical regulations, controlled substance policies, and hospital governance requirements.
