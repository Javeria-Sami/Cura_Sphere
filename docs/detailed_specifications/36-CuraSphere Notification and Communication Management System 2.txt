5.13.10 Functional Requirement NCM-FR-006
Internal Staff Messaging

Requirement ID
NCM-FR-006
Requirement Name
Internal Staff Messaging System

Description
The system shall provide a secure internal messaging platform for communication between hospital staff without requiring external messaging applications.
The messaging system shall support individual, departmental, and group communication while maintaining confidentiality and auditability.

Pharmacist

Radiology Staff

Business Rules

Only authenticated users shall exchange internal messages.

5.13.18 Functional Requirement NCM-FR-014
Notification Non-Functional Requirements
The Notification System shall satisfy the following quality requirements.
Performance
Critical notifications should typically be generated within 5 seconds of the triggering event.
Standard notifications should be queued and delivered without unnecessary delay.
Availability
Target availability: 99.9%, excluding planned maintenance.
Reliability
Notification failures shall not affect the originating clinical or business transaction.
Scalability
Support thousands of concurrent notifications.
Security
All notification traffic shall be encrypted where supported by the communication channel.
Backup & Recovery
Notification templates, schedules, and delivery history shall be included in system backup procedures.
