5.103 MODULE GROUP PURPOSE
The OPD and Electronic Medical Record modules shall manage the clinical care information generated when an eligible healthcare professional evaluates and treats a patient.
The modules shall establish a controlled clinical workflow from patient consultation entry to longitudinal medical record availability.
The primary relationship is:
PATIENT   │   ▼APPOINTMENT / WALK-IN   │   ▼CHECK-IN   │   ▼QUEUE   │   ▼CLINICAL ENCOUNTER   │   ├── Chief Complaint   ├── Clinical History   ├── Vital Signs   ├── Examination   ├── Clinical Notes   ├── Diagnosis   ├── Orders   ├── Prescription   └── Follow-Up Plan   │   ▼FINALIZED CLINICAL RECORD   │   ▼ELECTRONIC MEDICAL RECORD
The OPD module shall primarily manage the active outpatient consultation workflow.
The EMR module shall provide the authorized longitudinal clinical record of the patient.
The conceptual boundary is:
OPD │ ▼WHAT IS HAPPENINGIN THIS CONSULTATION?
Compared with:
EMR │ ▼WHAT CLINICAL INFORMATIONHAS BEEN RECORDED ACROSSTHE PATIENT'S CARE HISTORY?

5.104 CRITICAL CLINICAL ARCHITECTURE CONCEPT
CuraSphere shall distinguish the following concepts:
PATIENT
APPOINTMENT
QUEUE ENTRY
CLINICAL ENCOUNTER
MEDICAL RECORD
These concepts shall not be treated as identical.
Conceptually:
PATIENT   │   ▼Long-Term Person IdentityAPPOINTMENT   │   ▼Scheduled ReservationQUEUE ENTRY   │   ▼Operational Waiting StateCLINICAL ENCOUNTER   │   ▼Specific Care InteractionEMR   │   ▼Longitudinal Clinical Record
Example:
PATIENTMRN: MRN-000102Name: Patient A        │        ├──────────────────────────────┐        │                              │        ▼                              ▼ENCOUNTER 1                    ENCOUNTER 214 JUL 2026                   20 AUG 2026Cardiology                    General MedicineChest Pain                    Fever
The patient has one longitudinal identity.
The patient may have many clinical encounters.
Therefore:
ONE PATIENT     │     ▼MANY ENCOUNTERS
The Electronic Medical Record shall provide authorized access to clinical information associated with those encounters.

5.106 CLINICAL CONSULTATION START
CS-OPD-FR-001 — Start Clinical Consultation
Requirement Name
Start Clinical Consultation
Module
OPD — Outpatient Clinical Encounter Management
Description
The system shall allow an authorized clinical user to begin a consultation for an eligible patient.
Primary Actor
Doctor
Priority
Must Have
Preconditions
The clinical user is authenticated.
The user has consultation initiation permission.
The patient exists.
The patient is eligible for the applicable consultation.
The applicable operational context exists.
The patient identity is available.
Trigger
The doctor initiates consultation from the applicable worklist.
Main Flow
DOCTOR WORKLIST       │       ▼SELECT PATIENT       │       ▼VALIDATE QUEUE / CONSULTATION CONTEXT       │       ▼VERIFY PATIENT IDENTITY CONTEXT       │       ▼VALIDATE DOCTOR AUTHORITY       │       ▼CREATE OR OPEN ENCOUNTER       │       ▼MARK CONSULTATION ACTIVE       │       ▼OPEN CLINICAL WORKSPACE
Main Requirements
The system shall:
identify the selected patient;
validate the applicable consultation context;
validate the clinical user’s authority;
create or retrieve the eligible encounter;
establish the consultation state;
present the permitted clinical workspace.
Business Rules
CS-OPD-BR-001
A consultation shall reference an identifiable patient.
CS-OPD-BR-002
An active outpatient consultation shall reference an eligible clinical encounter.
CS-OPD-BR-003
The clinical user initiating the consultation shall have appropriate authorization.
CS-OPD-BR-004
The system shall prevent an invalid patient context switch during an active clinical workflow.
Critical Patient Safety Requirement
The system shall maintain visible patient identity context within the clinical workspace.
The clinical UI shall later consider persistent display of appropriate patient identifiers.
Conceptually:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐│ PATIENT: Ahmad Khan    MRN: MRN-000102    DOB: 12 APR 1980 │├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤│                                                              ││                    CLINICAL WORKSPACE                        ││                                                              │└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
The exact UI shall be designed later.
Acceptance Criteria
Given an authorized doctor and eligible patient consultation context, the doctor shall be able to begin the consultation and access the correct patient’s clinical workspace.

Important Architecture Principle
An encounter shall not be identified only by:
patient_id + date
Why?
A patient may have multiple care interactions on the same date.
Example:
14 JUL 202609:00Cardiology Consultation14:00Emergency Visit
Therefore, the encounter requires its own identity.

Requirement Name

Description

Priority
Must Have
Definition
The chief complaint represents the primary reason or concern that brought the patient for the current clinical consultation.
Examples
Chest pain for 2 hoursFever for 3 daysPersistent headacheShortness of breathAbdominal pain
Main Flow
Doctor opens an active encounter.
Doctor accesses the chief complaint section.
Doctor enters the complaint information.
System validates the encounter state.
System records the complaint in the encounter context.

Business Rules
CS-OPD-BR-010

CS-OPD-BR-011

The chief complaint shall not be stored directly as the permanent patient’s only complaint.

PATIENT TABLEpatient_idnamechief_complaint

Because:

The complaint belongs to the clinical encounter.
Conceptually:
PATIENT   │   ▼ENCOUNTER   │   ▼CHIEF COMPLAINT

5.155 CLINICAL MODULE RISK ANALYSIS
Risk 1 — Wrong Patient Clinical Documentation
Problem
A doctor opens Patient A.
The React state changes incorrectly.
Clinical documentation is submitted against Patient B.
Impact
Severe patient safety and clinical record integrity risk.
Mitigation
The backend shall validate:
ENCOUNTER    │    ▼PATIENT RELATIONSHIP
for protected clinical operations.
Persistent patient context shall be considered in Clinical UI Design.

Risk 2 — Finalized Notes Silently Overwritten
Problem
A finalized clinical note can be freely edited.
Impact
The historical clinical record becomes unreliable.
Mitigation
Use:
DRAFT EDITING        +FINALIZATION        +CONTROLLED ADDENDUM / CORRECTION

Risk 3 — Unknown Allergy Treated as No Allergy
Problem
The database contains no allergy records.
The UI displays:
NO ALLERGIES
Impact
Clinical staff may incorrectly assume the patient’s allergy status was verified.
Mitigation
Distinguish:
KNOWN ALLERGYNO KNOWN ALLERGYUNKNOWN

Risk 4 — Appointment Used as Medical Record
Problem
Developers store diagnosis and clinical notes directly in the appointment record.
Impact
Clinical and administrative domains become tightly coupled.
Mitigation
Use:
APPOINTMENT     │     ▼ENCOUNTER     │     ▼CLINICAL RECORD

Risk 5 — One Diagnosis Column
Problem
The encounter table contains:
diagnosis varchar(500)
Impact
Multiple diagnoses, diagnosis coding, primary diagnosis, and diagnosis history become difficult.
Mitigation
Model diagnosis as a clinical entity related to the encounter.

Risk 6 — EMR as One Giant Table
Problem
Developers create:
medical_recordpatient_namedoctor_namecomplainttemperatureblood_pressurediagnosismedicinelab_testxraynote
Impact
The system becomes difficult to normalize, query, audit, and extend.
Mitigation
The EMR shall be treated as a longitudinal clinical capability composed from clinical domains.

Risk 7 — React Hides Button but API Allows Action
Problem
React hides Finalize Encounter from a receptionist.
The API still accepts the finalization request.
Mitigation
ASP.NET Core authorization shall protect the use case.

Risk 8 — Clinical History Lost During Patient Merge
Problem
Duplicate patient records are merged incorrectly.
Historical encounters are deleted.
Mitigation
Patient merge workflows shall preserve clinical relationships and traceability.

Risk 9 — Clinical Orders Mixed with Specialized Workflow
Problem
OPD directly stores laboratory processing and radiology reporting logic.
Impact
OPD becomes a giant tightly coupled module.
Mitigation
Use explicit module boundaries:
OPD │ ▼ORDER REQUEST │ ├──► LAB ├──► RAD └──► PHA

Risk 10 — No Clinical Provenance
Problem
The system stores:
Diagnosis: Hypertension
but cannot answer:
Who recorded it?When?During which encounter?Was it later corrected?
Mitigation
Clinically significant information shall maintain appropriate provenance.
