6.13 Scalability Requirements
6.13.1 Introduction
The Scalability Requirements define how the CuraSphere Hospital Management System shall expand its capacity to support increasing numbers of users, hospitals, departments, services, data, and transactions without requiring major architectural changes.
The system shall be designed using a modular and extensible architecture to accommodate future growth while maintaining acceptable performance, reliability, and availability.
Scalability shall be supported at the application, database, infrastructure, and integration levels.

6.13.2 Objectives
The Scalability Requirements shall ensure that the system:
Supports future hospital expansion.
Accommodates increasing patient volumes.
Handles higher transaction loads.
Supports multiple hospital branches.
Supports distributed deployments.
Enables infrastructure growth with minimal downtime.
Maintains consistent performance during expansion.
Reduces the need for application redesign.

Distributed workload

NFR-SCL-001

6.13.14 Scalability Acceptance Criteria
The system shall be accepted when:
Additional application servers can be added with minimal configuration changes.
The system maintains acceptable performance as user load increases within planned capacity.
Multiple hospitals and branches operate independently without data leakage.
Database performance remains acceptable under expected data growth.
Infrastructure expansion does not require major application redesign.
