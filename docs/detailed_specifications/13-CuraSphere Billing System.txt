5.340 MODULE PURPOSE
The Billing, Invoicing and Patient Financial Management System shall manage patient-related financial workflows generated from eligible hospital services and approved financial activities.
The module shall support:
chargeable service configuration;
charge capture;
patient financial accounts;
invoice creation;
invoice item management;
invoice finalization;
payment collection;
payment allocation;
partial payments;
outstanding balances;
receipts;
payment reversals;
refunds;
approved discounts;
approved financial adjustments;
invoice cancellation and credit workflows;
patient financial history;
financial audit traceability.
The high-level workflow shall be:
CLINICAL OR OPERATIONAL EVENT              │              ▼     CHARGEABLE EVENT?              │         ┌────┴────┐         │         │        NO        YES         │         │         ▼         ▼     NO CHARGE   CHARGE CAPTURE                       │                       ▼                 PATIENT ACCOUNT                       │                       ▼                    INVOICE                       │                       ▼                 INVOICE ITEMS                       │                       ▼               INVOICE FINALIZATION                       │                       ▼                 AMOUNT PAYABLE                       │                       ▼                    PAYMENT                       │                       ▼               PAYMENT ALLOCATION                       │                ┌──────┴──────┐                │             │                ▼             ▼          PARTIAL PAYMENT   FULL PAYMENT                │             │                ▼             ▼          BALANCE DUE         PAID                │             │                └──────┬──────┘                       ▼                    RECEIPT                       │                       ▼            PATIENT FINANCIAL HISTORY
The module shall provide traceability for the following questions:
WHICH PATIENT?      │      ▼WHICH ENCOUNTER?      │      ▼WHICH SERVICE OCCURRED?      │      ▼WHY WAS A CHARGE CREATED?      │      ▼WHICH CHARGE WAS INVOICED?      │      ▼WHAT AMOUNT WAS BILLED?      │      ▼WAS A DISCOUNT APPLIED?      │      ▼WHO AUTHORIZED THE DISCOUNT?      │      ▼HOW MUCH WAS PAID?      │      ▼WHICH PAYMENT SETTLED WHICH INVOICE?      │      ▼WHAT BALANCE REMAINS?      │      ▼WAS ANY PAYMENT REVERSED?      │      ▼WAS ANY REFUND ISSUED?      │      ▼WHO PERFORMED THE FINANCIAL ACTION?
Financial traceability shall be considered a fundamental CuraSphere requirement.

5.341 BILLING DOMAIN BOUNDARY
The Billing module shall manage patient financial workflow.
It shall not own clinical diagnosis, laboratory interpretation, radiology reporting, medication prescribing, or clinical treatment decisions.
Conceptually:
OPD │ ├──────────────┐ │              │ ▼              │SERVICE EVENT   │                │IPD             │ │              │ ▼              │SERVICE EVENT   │                │LABORATORY      │ │              │ ▼              │LAB SERVICE     │                ├──────────────► BILLINGRADIOLOGY       │                     │ │              │                     ├── Charge Capture ▼              │                     ├── Patient AccountIMAGING SERVICE │                     ├── Invoice                │                     ├── PaymentPHARMACY        │                     ├── Allocation │              │                     ├── Receipt ▼              │                     ├── RefundDISPENSING      │                     └── Adjustment                │OTHER SERVICES  │ │              │ ▼              │SERVICE EVENT ──┘
Responsibilities shall conceptually be divided as follows:
CLINICAL MODULEAnswers:"What clinical service,order, procedure, ortreatment occurred?"
BILLING MODULEAnswers:"Does the eligible eventcreate a financial charge,and how is the patient'sfinancial obligation managed?"
PAYMENT WORKFLOWAnswers:"What money was received,through which method,and against which financialobligation?"
FINANCIAL AUDITAnswers:"Which actions explain thecurrent financial state?"
The Billing module shall not be designed as:
patient_idtotal_billpaid
This model is insufficient for a real hospital financial system.

5.342 CRITICAL BILLING ARCHITECTURE CONCEPT
CuraSphere shall distinguish:
SERVICE DEFINITION
PRICE CONFIGURATION
CHARGE
PATIENT FINANCIAL ACCOUNT
INVOICE
INVOICE ITEM
PAYMENT
PAYMENT ALLOCATION
RECEIPT
REFUND
FINANCIAL ADJUSTMENT
These concepts shall not automatically be treated as identical.
Conceptually:
SERVICE DEFINITION        │        ▼WHAT SERVICE EXISTS?
PRICE CONFIGURATION        │        ▼WHAT PRICING RULE ORAMOUNT APPLIES?
CHARGE        │        ▼WHAT FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONWAS GENERATED BY ANELIGIBLE EVENT?
PATIENT FINANCIAL ACCOUNT        │        ▼WHERE IS THE PATIENT'SFINANCIAL ACTIVITY GROUPED?
INVOICE        │        ▼WHAT AMOUNT WAS FORMALLYPRESENTED FOR PAYMENT?
INVOICE ITEM        │        ▼WHICH CHARGES OR FINANCIALLINES EXPLAIN THE INVOICE?
PAYMENT        │        ▼WHAT MONEY WAS RECEIVED?
PAYMENT ALLOCATION        │        ▼WHERE WAS THE PAYMENTAMOUNT APPLIED?
RECEIPT        │        ▼WHAT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OFPAYMENT WAS ISSUED?
REFUND        │        ▼WHAT ELIGIBLE MONEY WASRETURNED?
Therefore:
SERVICE   =CHARGE   =INVOICE   =PAYMENT   =RECEIPT
is an incorrect architectural assumption.

5.343 SERVICE EVENT VS FINANCIAL CHARGE
This distinction is fundamental.
Example:
CLINICAL EVENTLaboratory CBC Test Ordered
The clinical module records:
LAB ORDERLAB-ORD-00192
Billing may generate:
CHARGECHG-000501
The charge may reference:
SOURCE TYPELAB_ORDER
SOURCE REFERENCELAB-ORD-00192
Conceptually:
LAB ORDER    │    ▼ELIGIBLE BILLING EVENT    │    ▼CHARGE
The clinical event answers:
What happened clinically?
The charge answers:
What financial obligation was generated?
The system shall preserve this distinction.
Weak architecture:
lab_order.price = 1500lab_order.paid = true
This tightly couples laboratory workflow with financial workflow.
A stronger architecture is:
LABORATORY DOMAIN        │        ▼LAB ORDER        │        ▼BILLING INTEGRATION CONTRACT        │        ▼CHARGE        │        ▼INVOICE        │        ▼PAYMENT

CS-BIL-FR-002 — Create Chargeable Service
The system shall allow an authorized user to create an eligible chargeable service.
Main Flow

CS-BIL-FR-003 — Update Chargeable Service
The system shall allow authorized users to update eligible chargeable service configuration.
Historical financial records shall preserve applicable service context.
The following shall not occur:
OLD INVOICECBC = 1,200 PKR
Administrator changes the current service name or configuration.
Historical invoice unexpectedly changes its original financial meaning.
Historical financial context shall remain appropriately preserved.

CS-BIL-FR-004 — Activate or Deactivate Chargeable Service
The system shall allow authorized users to activate or deactivate eligible chargeable services.
ACTIVE   │   ▼AVAILABLE FOR ELIGIBLENEW CHARGE WORKFLOW
INACTIVE   │   ▼NOT NORMALLY AVAILABLEFOR NEW CHARGES   │   ▼HISTORICAL RECORDS REMAIN
Deactivation shall not delete historical charges.

CS-BIL-FR-005 — Search Chargeable Services
Authorized users shall be able to search chargeable services using applicable criteria.
Search criteria may include:
service code;
service name;
service category;
source domain;
active status;
chargeable status.

5.347 PRICE CONFIGURATION
CS-BIL-FR-006 — Maintain Price Configuration
Description
The system shall maintain controlled pricing information for eligible chargeable services.
Priority
Must Have
Critical Architecture Principle
The following design is weak:
servicesidnameprice
Why?
Suppose:
CBC PRICE01 JAN 2026PKR 1,000
The hospital changes the price:
01 JUL 2026PKR 1,200
If the developer executes:
UPDATE servicesSET price = 1200WHERE service_code = 'LAB-CBC';
and historical invoices dynamically read the current service price, an old invoice may incorrectly appear as:
PKR 1,200
instead of:
PKR 1,000
A stronger concept is:
CHARGEABLE SERVICE        │        ▼PRICE CONFIGURATION        │        ├── Amount        ├── Currency        ├── Effective From        ├── Effective To        └── Status

CS-BIL-FR-007 — Create Service Price
The system shall allow an authorized user to create an eligible price configuration for a chargeable service.
Price Information May Include
service;
amount;
currency;
effective from;
effective to;
price category;
configuration status.
Example
SERVICELAB-CBCAMOUNT1,200CURRENCYPKREFFECTIVE FROM2026-07-01

CS-BIL-FR-008 — Update Eligible Service Price
The system shall allow authorized users to update eligible price configuration according to approved financial policy.
Important Principle
A price already used for a completed historical financial transaction shall not be silently rewritten in a manner that changes the historical transaction.
A new price period may be required.
Conceptually:
PRICE VERSION 1PKR 1,00001 JAN 2026 → 30 JUN 2026
PRICE VERSION 2PKR 1,20001 JUL 2026 → CURRENT

CS-BIL-FR-009 — Maintain Price Effective Period
The system shall support applicable effective periods for service price configuration.
Conceptual Model
PRICE │ ├── EFFECTIVE_FROM └── EFFECTIVE_TO
The final temporal pricing architecture shall be defined during Billing Domain Design.
Business Rule
CS-BIL-BR-004
The system shall determine the applicable eligible price according to approved pricing rules and financial event context.

CS-BIL-FR-010 — Preserve Historical Price Context
The system shall preserve the financial price context applied to a historical charge or invoice item.
Example
Current catalog price:
PKR 1,500
Historical charge:
CHARGE DATE01 MAY 2026APPLIED UNIT PRICEPKR 1,000
The historical charge remains:
PKR 1,000
It shall not automatically become:
PKR 1,500
Business Rule
CS-BIL-BR-005
Historical transaction values shall not be recalculated using unrelated future price changes.

5.354 CHARGE GROSS AMOUNT
CS-BIL-FR-022 — Calculate Charge Gross Amount
The system shall calculate the gross charge amount according to approved financial calculation rules.
Conceptually:
GROSS AMOUNT      =QUANTITY      ×UNIT PRICE
Example:
3 × 5,000     │     ▼15,000 PKR
Important Requirement
Financial calculation shall use appropriate decimal monetary representation.
The system shall not rely on binary floating-point values for authoritative monetary calculations where decimal precision is required.
Conceptually in C#:
decimal
shall be evaluated for financial amounts rather than:
float
or careless use of:
double
The exact monetary precision strategy shall be defined during Data Architecture.

5.400 INVOICE RELATIONSHIP MODEL
PATIENT FINANCIAL ACCOUNT           │           ▼         INVOICE           │           ▼      INVOICE ITEMS           │           ▼         CHARGES

ONE INVOICE     │     ▼ONE OR MORE INVOICE ITEMS

invoiceservice1amount1service2amount2service3amount3

5.401 PAYMENT ALLOCATION RELATIONSHIP MODEL
PAYMENT   │   ▼PAYMENT ALLOCATION   │   ▼INVOICE

ONE PAYMENT    │    ▼MULTIPLE ALLOCATIONS
and potentially:
ONE INVOICE    │    ▼MULTIPLE PAYMENT ALLOCATIONS
Example:
INV-10010,000   │   ├── PAY-001 → 4,000   ├── PAY-002 → 3,000   └── PAY-003 → 3,000
Therefore:
INVOICE ≠ PAYMENT

5.409 BILLING MODULE RISK ANALYSIS
Risk 1 — Clinical Service and Charge Are the Same Record
Problem
The laboratory order contains:
pricepaidreceipt_number
Impact
Clinical and financial domains become tightly coupled.
Mitigation
Separate source clinical events from billing charges.

Risk 2 — Current Service Price Rewrites History
Problem
The price catalog changes from:
1,000
to:
1,500
Old invoices dynamically show the new price.
Impact
Historical financial records become inaccurate.
Mitigation
Preserve applied transaction price context.

Risk 3 — Duplicate Charge From Integration Retry
Problem
A laboratory event is sent twice.
Two charges are created.
Impact
The patient is overbilled.
Mitigation
Implement source event traceability and idempotent charge capture.

Risk 4 — Invoice Total Entered Manually
Problem
User enters:
Total = 20,000
Invoice items actually total:
17,500
Impact
Financial inconsistency.
Mitigation
Calculate authoritative totals using trusted backend rules.

Risk 5 — Money Stored Using Inappropriate Floating Point
Problem
Financial values use careless binary floating-point arithmetic.
Impact
Rounding and precision errors may occur.
Mitigation
Use approved decimal monetary representation and rounding policy.

Risk 6 — Boolean Paid Field
Problem
paid = true
Impact
Partial payment cannot be represented.
Mitigation
Use payment transactions, allocations, balances, and invoice states.

Risk 7 — Payment and Invoice Are the Same Entity
Problem
Each invoice has:
payment_amountpayment_methodpayment_date
and assumes exactly one payment.
Impact
Multiple payments and multi-invoice allocations become difficult.
Mitigation
Separate Payment and Payment Allocation.

Risk 8 — Payment Saved Without Allocation
Problem
Payment is saved.
System fails before invoice allocation.
Impact
Cash exists in the system but no obligation reflects payment.
Mitigation
Use appropriate transaction boundaries.

Risk 9 — Invoice Marked Paid Without Payment
Problem
A user directly changes:
status = 'PAID'
Impact
No payment explains the paid status.
Mitigation
Derive or control invoice financial state from valid financial workflow.

Risk 10 — Discounts Controlled by React
Problem
The browser allows:
discount = 100%
Impact
A malicious request bypasses UI restrictions.
Mitigation
Backend authorization and discount validation.

Risk 11 — Payment Deleted During Correction
Problem
Cashier deletes an incorrect payment.
Impact
Financial audit history disappears.
Mitigation
Use payment reversal.

Risk 12 — Refund Exceeds Paid Amount
Problem
Patient paid:
5,000
System refunds:
8,000
Impact
Invalid financial loss.
Mitigation
Calculate and validate refundable financial context.

Risk 13 — Receipt Reprint Creates New Payment
Problem
Each receipt print action inserts another payment.
Impact
Financial totals increase incorrectly.
Mitigation
Separate receipt representation from payment transaction.

Risk 14 — Patient Balance Calculated Only in React
Problem
React downloads records and calculates the balance.
Impact
Different clients may display inconsistent financial values.
Mitigation
Trusted backend financial calculations and reporting projections.

Risk 15 — Financial Records Physically Deleted
Problem
Charges, invoices, and payments are deleted to “fix” errors.
Impact
Financial traceability is destroyed.
Mitigation
Use controlled cancellation, reversal, credit, refund, and adjustment workflows.

Risk 16 — Tax Rules Invented by Developers
Problem
A developer hardcodes an assumed tax percentage.
Impact
Incorrect legal and financial calculations.
Mitigation
Use approved financial and legal policy configuration.

Risk 17 — Digital Payment Browser Success Treated as Payment Confirmation
Problem
Frontend receives a success page and marks the invoice paid.
Impact
A failed or manipulated payment may appear completed.
Mitigation
Backend provider verification and approved callback processing.

Risk 18 — No Financial Authorization Separation
Problem
One cashier can:
CREATE PAYMENTREVERSE PAYMENTAPPROVE REFUNDAUTHORIZE DISCOUNTCREATE ADJUSTMENT
Impact
Fraud and financial control risk increases.
Mitigation
Use permission separation and approval workflows.
