1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
The purpose of CuraSphere is to provide a centralized, secure, modular, and scalable Hospital Management System for managing the clinical, administrative, operational, and financial activities of a healthcare organization.
CuraSphere is intended to replace fragmented manual processes, paper-based records, isolated spreadsheets, and disconnected departmental software with a unified digital platform.
The system will support the complete operational flow of a hospital, beginning with patient registration and continuing through appointments, clinical consultations, admissions, laboratory services, radiology, pharmacy, billing, discharge, and reporting.
The software shall provide authorized hospital personnel with access to accurate information according to their assigned responsibilities and permissions.
CuraSphere is designed as a long-term software product rather than a single-purpose academic application.
The product shall therefore emphasize:
modularity;
security;
scalability;
maintainability;
usability;
auditability;
data integrity;
operational reliability;
testability;
future integration capability.

1.2 Document Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification defines the functional and non-functional requirements of CuraSphere.
The SRS acts as the formal requirements baseline for the software.
It answers the following questions:
What shall CuraSphere do?
Who shall use CuraSphere?
What hospital processes shall the system support?
What business rules shall control system operations?
What security restrictions shall be applied?
What performance is expected from the software?
What information shall the system store?
What interfaces shall the system provide?
What quality characteristics shall the system maintain?
How shall requirements be traced to development and testing?
The SRS shall be used before and during:
UI/UX design;
software architecture design;
database design;
API design;
frontend development;
backend development;
DevOps implementation;
software testing;
deployment;
maintenance.
The following relationship shall be maintained:
BUSINESS NEED      │      ▼PROJECT VISION      │      ▼SOFTWARE REQUIREMENT      │      ▼USE CASE / USER STORY      │      ▼UI DESIGN      │      ▼SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE      │      ▼DATABASE + API      │      ▼IMPLEMENTATION      │      ▼TEST CASE      │      ▼DEPLOYED FEATURE
A feature should not be added to CuraSphere without understanding its business or system requirement.

1.4 Product Scope
CuraSphere shall provide an integrated hospital management platform covering four primary operational domains.
A. Clinical Management
The clinical domain shall support activities directly associated with patient healthcare.
It includes:
patient medical records;
doctor consultations;
OPD operations;
IPD operations;
emergency care;
nursing activities;
diagnosis records;
prescriptions;
clinical notes;
patient vitals;
procedures;
discharge processes.
B. Diagnostic and Medical Support Services
The diagnostic domain shall manage supporting medical services.
It includes:
laboratory test requests;
sample collection;
laboratory results;
radiology requests;
imaging reports;
pharmacy prescriptions;
medicine dispensing.
C. Administrative and Operational Management
The administrative domain shall support hospital operations.
It includes:
user management;
doctor management;
nurse management;
department management;
appointment scheduling;
room and bed management;
staff management;
inventory;
system configuration.
D. Financial and Management Services
The financial domain shall support:
billing;
invoices;
payments;
refunds;
insurance;
expenses;
financial reporting;
management analytics.

The vision of CuraSphere is:
To provide a secure, intelligent, scalable, and unified digital healthcare management platform that connects hospital departments and enables healthcare professionals to deliver efficient and patient-centered services.
CuraSphere shall evolve through controlled development iterations.

1.8 Document Conventions
The following requirement terminology shall be used throughout this document.
SHALL
The word shall represents a mandatory system requirement.
Example:
The system shall require authentication before allowing access to protected hospital information.
SHOULD
The word should represents a recommended requirement.
Example:
The dashboard should display the most important operational indicators first.
MAY
The word may represents an optional capability.
Example:
The hospital administrator may configure additional dashboard widgets.
MUST NOT
The phrase must not defines prohibited system behavior.
Example:
A receptionist must not modify a doctor’s clinical diagnosis.

1.9 References
The CuraSphere requirements process shall consider applicable software engineering, healthcare interoperability, security, accessibility, and software quality guidance during detailed design and implementation.
Reference categories include:
software requirements engineering standards;
software quality models;
healthcare information system practices;
healthcare interoperability principles;
RESTful API design practices;
secure software development practices;
accessibility guidance;
PostgreSQL technical documentation;
React technical documentation;
ASP.NET Core technical documentation;
Entity Framework Core technical documentation;
containerization and CI/CD documentation.
Specific regulatory and healthcare compliance requirements shall be selected according to the country, hospital, and deployment jurisdiction before production deployment.
CuraSphere shall not automatically claim compliance with a healthcare regulation merely because a security feature has been implemented.
Regulatory compliance requires formal legal, technical, organizational, and operational assessment.
