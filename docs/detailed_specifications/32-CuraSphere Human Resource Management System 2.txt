5.11.10 Functional Requirement HRM-FR-006
Leave Management

Requirement ID
HRM-FR-006
Requirement Name
Employee Leave Management

Description
The system shall manage all employee leave requests, approvals, balances, and leave history according to hospital HR policies.
The Leave Management System shall support different leave categories, approval workflows, leave balance calculations, and integration with attendance and payroll.

Actors
Employee
Department Manager
HR Officer
HR Manager
Payroll Officer

Preconditions
Employee must be active.
Employee must have an assigned leave policy.
Leave balance must be available (where applicable).

Trigger
Employee submits a leave request.

Main Workflow
Employee Requests Leave
          │
          ▼
Leave Balance Validation
          │
          ▼
Manager Approval
          │
     ┌────┴────┐
     │         │
 Approved   Rejected
     │
     ▼
HR Verification
     │
     ▼
Attendance Updated
     │
     ▼
Payroll Updated
     │
     ▼
Audit Log Created

Supported Leave Types
The system shall support configurable leave categories, including:
Annual Leave
Casual Leave
Sick Leave
Maternity Leave
Paternity Leave
Emergency Leave
Study Leave
Unpaid Leave
Compensatory Leave
Bereavement Leave
Public Holiday
Special Leave

Leave Request Information
The system shall record:
Leave Request ID
Employee ID
Leave Type
Start Date
End Date
Total Days
Reason
Attachment (Medical Certificate if applicable)
Approval Status
Approver
Approval Date
Comments

Business Rules
HRM-BR-020
Employees cannot request leave exceeding available leave balance unless the leave type permits negative balances or unpaid leave.
HRM-BR-021
Approved leave shall automatically update attendance records.
HRM-BR-022
Rejected leave shall not affect attendance or payroll.
HRM-BR-023
Medical leave beyond configurable limits may require supporting documentation.
HRM-BR-024
Leave requests overlapping with existing approved leave shall be rejected.

Acceptance Criteria
Leave requests processed electronically.
Leave balances updated automatically.
Attendance synchronized.
Payroll notified.
Audit generated.

5.11.20 Functional Requirement HRM-FR-016
HR Non-Functional Requirements
The HR module shall provide:
Performance
Employee searches should typically complete within 2 seconds under normal operating conditions.
Attendance synchronization should occur immediately after successful check-in or check-out.
Availability
Target availability: 99.9%, excluding planned maintenance.
Security
Multi-factor authentication may be enabled for HR administrators.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall restrict HR functions.
Reliability
Payroll-related operations shall use transactional processing to ensure consistency.
Scalability
The architecture shall support thousands of employees across multiple hospital branches.
Backup & Recovery
Employee records shall be included in scheduled backup and disaster recovery procedures.
