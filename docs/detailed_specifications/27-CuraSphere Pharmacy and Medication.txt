Part 5.9 - Pharmacy and Medication Management System (PHR)

5.9.1 Introduction
The Pharmacy and Medication Management System (PHR) is responsible for managing the complete medication lifecycle within the CuraSphere Hospital Management System.
This module ensures that medications prescribed by authorized healthcare professionals are accurately validated, dispensed, tracked, billed, monitored, and recorded while maintaining patient safety and regulatory compliance.
The Pharmacy System integrates with multiple CuraSphere modules, including:
Electronic Medical Record (EMR)
Computerized Physician Order Entry (CPOE)
Outpatient Department (OPD)
Inpatient Department (IPD)
Emergency Department (ED)
Laboratory
Billing System
Inventory Management
Procurement System
Drug Formulary
Insurance System
Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)
Audit Management
The system shall minimize medication errors, improve dispensing efficiency, ensure medicine traceability, and maintain complete pharmaceutical records.

5.9.2 Objectives
The Pharmacy module shall achieve the following objectives.
Manage hospital medicine inventory.
Maintain complete medicine master records.
Process electronic prescriptions.
Reduce medication errors.
Improve dispensing accuracy.
Track medicine batches.
Monitor medicine expiry.
Prevent dispensing expired medicines.
Manage controlled medicines.
Record medication history.
Integrate pharmacy with billing.
Support insurance medication coverage.
Improve patient safety.
Maintain complete audit history.
Generate pharmacy reports.

5.9.5 Functional Requirement PHR-FR-001
Medicine Master Management

Requirement ID
PHR-FR-001
Requirement Name
Medicine Master Management

Description
The system shall maintain a centralized Medicine Master containing every medicine approved for use within the hospital.
Every medicine shall possess a unique identifier and standardized pharmaceutical information.

Actors
Pharmacist
Inventory Officer
Pharmacy Manager
System Administrator

Preconditions
User must have pharmacy management permission.
Pharmacy configuration must be active.

Trigger
Authorized user creates or updates medicine information.

Main Flow
Open Medicine Master
        │
        ▼
Create / Edit Medicine
        │
        ▼
Validate Information
        │
        ▼
Save Medicine
        │
        ▼
Assign Medicine ID
        │
        ▼
Audit Record Created

System shall store

Generic Name
Brand Name
Strength
Dosage Form
Manufacturer
Supplier
Drug Class
Therapeutic Category
Route of Administration
Storage Conditions
Unit Price
Selling Price
Barcode
Status

Alternative Flow
Existing medicine updated.

Exception Flow
Duplicate medicine detected.
System shall reject duplicate entries.

Business Rules
PHR-BR-001
Every medicine shall have one unique Medicine ID.
PHR-BR-002
Medicine records shall never be permanently deleted.
PHR-BR-003
Only authorized pharmacy administrators may modify medicine information.

Acceptance Criteria
✔ Medicine created successfully
✔ Duplicate medicine prevented
✔ Audit generated
