5.496 MODULE PURPOSE
The Human Resources, Staff, Duty Roster and Workforce Management System shall manage the controlled administrative and operational lifecycle of hospital staff.
The module shall support:
employee identity management;
staff profile management;
employee number generation;
employment information;
department assignment;
designation assignment;
staff category management;
doctor workforce context;
nurse workforce context;
non-clinical staff context;
professional credential records;
credential expiry monitoring;
employment status management;
staff transfer history;
shift definition;
shift scheduling;
duty roster creation;
duty roster approval;
staff shift assignment;
shift conflict validation;
attendance recording;
attendance correction;
leave type management;
leave request;
leave approval;
leave rejection;
staff availability;
workforce history;
HR document metadata;
workforce search;
workforce reporting;
HR audit events.
The high-level workforce lifecycle shall be:
PERSON   │   ▼EMPLOYEE REGISTRATION   │   ▼STAFF PROFILE   │   ▼EMPLOYMENT RECORD   │   ▼DEPARTMENT ASSIGNMENT   │   ▼DESIGNATION ASSIGNMENT   │   ▼PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIALS   │   ▼ACTIVE EMPLOYMENT   │   ▼DUTY ROSTER   │   ▼SHIFT ASSIGNMENT   │   ▼ATTENDANCE   │   ├───────────────┐   │               │   ▼               ▼LEAVE          DUTY HISTORY   │               │   ▼               ▼APPROVAL       WORKFORCE RECORD   │   └───────┬───────┘           ▼    STAFF AVAILABILITY
The module shall preserve workforce traceability for:
WHO IS THE EMPLOYEE?        ↓WHAT IS THE EMPLOYEE NUMBER?        ↓WHAT IS THE EMPLOYMENT STATUS?        ↓WHICH DEPARTMENT?        ↓WHICH DESIGNATION?        ↓WHAT PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIALS?        ↓WHICH SHIFT?        ↓WHICH DUTY ROSTER?        ↓WAS THE EMPLOYEE PRESENT?        ↓WAS THE EMPLOYEE ON APPROVED LEAVE?        ↓WHAT IS THE CURRENT AVAILABILITY?        ↓WHAT CHANGED IN THE EMPLOYEE HISTORY?

5.497 HUMAN RESOURCES DOMAIN BOUNDARY
The HRM/WFM module shall own administrative workforce information and hospital workforce scheduling.
It shall not own:
patient clinical records;
diagnosis;
treatment plans;
prescriptions;
laboratory results;
radiology reports;
patient billing;
medication stock;
general ledger accounting.
Conceptually:
HUMAN RESOURCES       │       ▼MANAGE EMPLOYMENT       │       ▼WORKFORCE MANAGEMENT       │       ▼MANAGE DUTY AVAILABILITY       │       ├────────────► DOCTOR WORKFORCE       │       ├────────────► NURSING WORKFORCE       │       ├────────────► LABORATORY STAFF       │       ├────────────► RADIOLOGY STAFF       │       ├────────────► PHARMACY STAFF       │       ├────────────► ADMINISTRATIVE STAFF       │       └────────────► SUPPORT STAFF
The Doctor Management or Clinical module may answer:
Which doctor treated the patient?
The HR module answers:
What is the doctor’s employment and organizational context?
The Appointment module may answer:
Which doctor is booked for an appointment?
The Workforce module answers:
Is the staff member assigned to an applicable duty schedule?
The Authentication module may answer:
Which user account is signed in?
The HR module answers:
Which employee or staff record is associated with that account?
Therefore:
USER ACCOUNT      ≠EMPLOYEE      ≠CLINICAL PRACTITIONER PROFILE      ≠SHIFT ASSIGNMENT

5.498 CRITICAL WORKFORCE ARCHITECTURE CONCEPT
CuraSphere shall distinguish:
PERSON
USER ACCOUNT
EMPLOYEE
EMPLOYMENT RECORD
DEPARTMENT ASSIGNMENT
DESIGNATION ASSIGNMENT
PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIAL
SHIFT
DUTY ROSTER
SHIFT ASSIGNMENT
ATTENDANCE RECORD
LEAVE REQUEST
STAFF AVAILABILITY
These concepts shall not be collapsed into one staff table.
Weak architecture:
staffnameemailpasswordroledepartmentdesignationshiftattendanceleave_status
This design cannot reliably answer:
WHEN DID THE EMPLOYEE JOIN?WAS THE EMPLOYEE TRANSFERRED?WHAT WAS THE PREVIOUS DEPARTMENT?WHAT WAS THE PREVIOUS DESIGNATION?WHICH ROSTER ASSIGNED THE SHIFT?WHO APPROVED THE ROSTER?WAS THE EMPLOYEE ON LEAVE?WHEN DID THE CREDENTIAL EXPIRE?WHY IS THE EMPLOYEE UNAVAILABLE?
A stronger conceptual architecture is:
PERSON   │   ▼EMPLOYEE   │   ├────────────► EMPLOYMENT RECORD   │   ├────────────► DEPARTMENT ASSIGNMENT   │   ├────────────► DESIGNATION ASSIGNMENT   │   ├────────────► PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIAL   │   ├────────────► SHIFT ASSIGNMENT   │   ├────────────► ATTENDANCE   │   └────────────► LEAVE

5.501 EMPLOYEE MASTER
CS-HRM-FR-001 — Maintain Employee Master
Description
The system shall maintain a controlled master record for each eligible hospital employee.
Priority
Must Have
Conceptual Employee Model
EMPLOYEE   │   ├── Employee ID   ├── Employee Number   ├── Person Reference   ├── Staff Category   ├── Employment Status   ├── Primary Department Context   ├── Current Designation Context   └── Active Workforce Context
Critical Principle
The employee master shall not unnecessarily duplicate all person identity information.
Conceptually:
PERSON  │  ▼EMPLOYEE
A person may contain general identity information.
The employee record contains workforce identity.
Example:
PERSONPER-000821
EMPLOYEEEMP-000421
The two identities shall remain distinguishable.
Business Rules
CS-HRM-BR-001
Each employee shall have a unique internal employee identity.
CS-HRM-BR-002
Each active employee number shall be unique within the applicable hospital scope.
CS-HRM-BR-003
A user account shall not automatically be treated as an employee record.

5.527 UPDATE SHIFT
CS-HRM-FR-028 — Update Shift
The system shall allow authorized users to update eligible shift definitions.
Changes to a shift definition shall not silently rewrite historical duty assignments.
Example:
Historical shift:
MORNING08:00 → 16:00
New policy:
MORNING07:00 → 15:00
Historical 2025 roster records shall not automatically appear as:
07:00 → 15:00
if the employee actually worked under the previous shift definition.
The final temporal shift configuration strategy shall be defined during Workforce Domain Architecture.

5.591 HRM/WFM RISK ANALYSIS
Risk 1 — User Account and Employee Are the Same Record
Problem
Authentication information and employment information are stored together.
Impact
Security and workforce lifecycle become tightly coupled.
Mitigation
Separate user account and employee concepts.

Risk 2 — One Staff Table Stores Everything
Problem
namedepartmentdesignationshiftattendanceleave
are stored in one table.
Impact
Historical and scheduling data become unreliable.
Mitigation
Separate employee, employment, assignment, roster, attendance, and leave concepts.

Risk 3 — Department Transfer Overwrites History
Problem
employee.department = "Cardiology"
Impact
Previous department assignment disappears.
Mitigation
Maintain effective-dated department assignment history.

Risk 4 — Designation Change Overwrites History
Problem
Employee designation is directly replaced.
Impact
Promotion and designation history cannot be reconstructed.
Mitigation
Maintain designation assignment history.

Risk 5 — Employee Shift Stored as One Field
Problem
employee.shift = "Morning"
Impact
Real duty scheduling cannot be represented.
Mitigation
Use duty roster and shift assignment concepts.

Risk 6 — Cross-Midnight Shift Is Invalidated
Problem
Night shift:
20:00 → 08:00
is treated as invalid because:
08:00 < 20:00
Impact
Hospital night duty cannot be represented correctly.
Mitigation
Use full date-time intervals.

Risk 7 — Shift Conflict Checked Only in React
Problem
React displays a warning.
Direct API request bypasses the warning.
Impact
An employee receives overlapping duties.
Mitigation
Perform authoritative conflict validation in ASP.NET Core.

Risk 8 — Duty Roster Treated as Attendance
Problem
Every scheduled employee is automatically marked present.
Impact
Attendance records become false.
Mitigation
Separate expected duty from actual attendance.

Risk 9 — Attendance Correction Deletes Original Value
Problem
Original:
09:15
is replaced by:
08:05
Impact
The system cannot explain the correction.
Mitigation
Preserve original and corrected attendance context.

Risk 10 — Approved Leave Is Ignored by Roster
Problem
Employee is on approved leave.
Roster assigns a night shift.
Impact
Workforce scheduling becomes unreliable.
Mitigation
Integrate approved leave into roster validation.

Risk 11 — Employment Status Is Ignored During Scheduling
Problem
A resigned employee is assigned to a future shift.
Impact
Invalid workforce coverage.
Mitigation
Validate employment eligibility for the duty date.

Risk 12 — Credential Expiry Is Not Monitored
Problem
Professional credential expires unnoticed.
Impact
Compliance and workforce risk.
Mitigation
Support credential expiry monitoring.

Risk 13 — Credential Expiry Automatically Terminates Employee
Problem
The system changes employment status to terminated.
Impact
Administrative policy is incorrectly automated.
Mitigation
Separate credential status from employment status and apply approved workforce policy.

Risk 14 — Staff Availability Is One Boolean
Problem
isAvailable = true
Impact
Leave, duty, employment, and credential context are ignored.
Mitigation
Derive availability from approved workforce facts.

Risk 15 — Leave Approval Controlled Only by UI
Problem
Approval button is hidden.
A user directly calls the approval API.
Impact
Unauthorized leave approval.
Mitigation
Backend authorization policy.

Risk 16 — HR Documents Are Public URLs
Problem
Anyone with a URL can access employee documents.
Impact
Sensitive workforce information exposure.
Mitigation
Use authorized document access architecture.

Risk 17 — Current Shift Definition Rewrites Historical Rosters
Problem
Morning shift changes from:
08:00 → 16:00
to:
07:00 → 15:00
All historical rosters display the new time.
Impact
Historical duty records become inaccurate.
Mitigation
Preserve applicable historical shift timing context.

Risk 18 — Attendance Is Automatically Used as Payroll
Problem
Attendance totals directly generate salary.
Impact
Payroll rules, allowances, tax, and authorized adjustments are ignored.
Mitigation
Define a separate payroll integration boundary.
