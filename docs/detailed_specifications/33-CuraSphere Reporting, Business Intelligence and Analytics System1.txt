CuraSphere - Hospital Management System
Section 5 - System Functional Requirements
Part 5.12 - Reporting, Business Intelligence and Analytics System (RBI)

5.12.1 Introduction
The Reporting, Business Intelligence and Analytics System (RBI) provides centralized operational, clinical, financial, and executive reporting for the CuraSphere Hospital Management System.
This module transforms data collected from all hospital departments into meaningful reports, dashboards, Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), statistical analysis, and executive insights to support operational monitoring and strategic decision-making.
Unlike operational modules, the RBI module does not directly create clinical or financial transactions. Instead, it consolidates information from integrated systems and presents it through reports, dashboards, visualizations, and analytics.
The Reporting and Business Intelligence System shall support real-time monitoring, historical analysis, regulatory reporting, and performance measurement.
