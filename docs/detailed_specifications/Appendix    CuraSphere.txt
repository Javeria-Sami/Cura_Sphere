Appendix A Glossary of Terms and Acronyms
A.1 Introduction
This appendix provides definitions of technical terms, medical terminology, abbreviations, and acronyms used throughout the CuraSphere Hospital Management System (HMS) Software Requirements Specification (SRS).
The glossary ensures that all stakeholders—including hospital administrators, healthcare professionals, developers, testers, project managers, auditors, and regulatory authorities—share a common understanding of the terminology used within the project.

A.2 Purpose
The glossary aims to:
Standardize terminology across the project.
Reduce ambiguity in documentation.
Improve communication among stakeholders.
Support onboarding of new team members.
Provide quick reference for technical and medical terms.
Ensure consistency throughout the SRS.
