Part 5.10 — Inventory and Medical Store Management System (INV)

5.10.1 Introduction
The Inventory and Medical Store Management System (INV) is the centralized module responsible for managing all hospital inventory, medical supplies, consumables, pharmaceuticals, laboratory reagents, surgical instruments, office supplies, biomedical equipment, and warehouse operations.
The Inventory Management System ensures that all hospital departments have the required supplies at the right time while minimizing shortages, overstocking, wastage, and financial loss.
This module integrates with almost every operational department of CuraSphere, making it one of the hospital's core operational systems.

5.10.2 Objectives
The Inventory System shall:
Maintain centralized inventory records.
Prevent stock shortages.
Prevent overstocking.
Track medicine batches.
Track expiry dates.
Manage suppliers.
Manage purchase orders.
Support multiple warehouses.
Maintain inventory valuation.
Reduce inventory wastage.
Support stock forecasting.
Integrate with Pharmacy.
Integrate with Laboratory.
Integrate with Billing.
Maintain complete inventory audit history.

5.10.5 Functional Requirement INV-FR-001
Inventory Master Management

Requirement ID
INV-FR-001
Requirement Name
Inventory Item Master Management

Description
The system shall maintain a centralized Inventory Master Database containing every stock item used within the hospital.
Each inventory item shall be uniquely identified and categorized.

Actors
Inventory Officer
Store Manager
Administrator

Preconditions
User has inventory management permissions.

Authorized user creates or updates an inventory item.

Business Rules
INV-BR-001
Each inventory item shall possess one unique Item Code.
INV-BR-002
Inventory records shall never be permanently deleted.
INV-BR-003
Only authorized inventory personnel may modify inventory information.

Acceptance Criteria
Inventory items can be created.
Duplicate Item Codes are prevented.
Audit records are generated.
