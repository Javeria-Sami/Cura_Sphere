6.19 Logging, Monitoring & Auditability Requirements

6.19.1 Introduction
The Logging, Monitoring, and Auditability requirements define how the CuraSphere Hospital Management System records operational activities, monitors application health, detects abnormal behavior, and provides complete traceability of user and system actions.
Hospitals process thousands of critical operations every day, including patient registrations, clinical documentation, laboratory testing, medication dispensing, billing, admissions, and emergency care. Every significant system event must be traceable for operational support, security investigations, regulatory compliance, troubleshooting, and performance optimization.
CuraSphere shall implement centralized logging, real-time monitoring, comprehensive audit trails, alerting mechanisms, and analytics dashboards to ensure complete operational visibility across the platform.

The purpose of this section is to establish enterprise-level requirements for:
Operational Logging

6.19.3 Objectives
The Logging and Monitoring framework shall:
Record all significant activities.
Detect failures quickly.
Monitor infrastructure continuously.
Improve incident response.
Support troubleshooting.
Detect suspicious activities.
Improve system availability.
Support compliance audits.
Provide operational dashboards.
Maintain complete traceability.

6.19.16 Business Rules
Rule ID
Rule
NFR-MON-001
Every critical transaction shall generate an audit record.
NFR-MON-002
System clocks shall use synchronized time sources.
NFR-MON-003
Logs shall not contain plain-text passwords or sensitive authentication secrets.
NFR-MON-004
Audit records shall be protected from unauthorized modification.
NFR-MON-005
Critical alerts shall be delivered immediately to designated administrators.
NFR-MON-006
Monitoring services shall continue operating independently of application modules where feasible.
NFR-MON-007
All log retention policies shall be configurable by authorized administrators.

6.19.17 Acceptance Criteria
The monitoring and logging solution shall be accepted when:
All defined system events are logged successfully.
Audit trails provide complete traceability.
Monitoring dashboards accurately display system health.
Critical alerts are generated within configured thresholds.
Health checks accurately report service status.
Log retention policies operate according to configuration.
Audit records remain protected from unauthorized alteration.

6.19.19 Section Summary
The Logging, Monitoring, and Auditability framework provides complete operational visibility into the CuraSphere Hospital Management System. By combining structured logging, comprehensive audit trails, proactive monitoring, real-time alerting, and centralized dashboards, the system enables rapid incident detection, efficient troubleshooting, regulatory compliance, and continuous operational improvement while maintaining the security and integrity of healthcare information.
