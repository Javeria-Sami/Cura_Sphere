5.10.10 Functional Requirement INV-FR-006
Warehouse Management

Requirement ID
INV-FR-006
Requirement Name
Warehouse Management

Description
The system shall support the management of one or more warehouses and storage locations throughout the hospital.
Each warehouse shall maintain independent inventory records while remaining synchronized with the centralized Inventory Management System.
The warehouse structure shall support:
Central Warehouse
Pharmacy Store
Laboratory Store
Operation Theatre Store
Emergency Store
ICU Store
Departmental Stores
Satellite Warehouses

Actors
Store Manager
Inventory Officer
Warehouse Supervisor
System Administrator

Preconditions
Warehouse must exist.
User must have warehouse permissions.

Trigger
Inventory is received, transferred, issued, adjusted, or counted.

5.10.22 Functional Requirement INV-FR-018
Inventory Non-Functional Requirements
The Inventory module shall satisfy the following:
Performance
Inventory searches should typically complete within 2 seconds under normal operating conditions.
Stock updates should occur immediately after confirmed transactions.
Availability
Target availability: 99.9% excluding planned maintenance.
Reliability
Inventory transactions shall use ACID-compliant database transactions to maintain consistency.
Security
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall restrict inventory operations.
Scalability
Support multiple hospitals, warehouses, and thousands of inventory items.
Backup & Recovery
Inventory records shall be included in disaster recovery and backup procedures.
Data Integrity
Duplicate item codes shall be prevented.
Negative inventory balances shall be blocked unless explicitly permitted by authorized policy.
