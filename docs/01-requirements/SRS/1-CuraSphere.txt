1. Executive Summary
JUH-Tech Health is a modern Hospital Management System (HMS) designed to digitize and integrate hospital operations into one secure, scalable platform.
The system will replace manual paperwork and disconnected software by providing a centralized solution for patient management, appointments, medical records, pharmacy, laboratory, billing, inventory, human resources, and administration.
The goal is to improve healthcare quality, operational efficiency, security, and decision-making through modern software engineering practices.

2. Introduction
Healthcare organizations generate and rely on large volumes of information every day. Managing patient records, appointments, billing, laboratory reports, pharmacy inventory, and staff manually or through disconnected systems often leads to delays, errors, and duplicated work.
JUH-Tech Health aims to solve these challenges by providing a unified software platform that enables healthcare professionals to work efficiently while ensuring that patient information remains secure, accurate, and easily accessible to authorized users.

3. What is a Hospital Management System (HMS)?
A Hospital Management System is an integrated software solution used to manage the clinical, administrative, financial, and operational activities of a healthcare organization.
It connects all departments through a shared database and standardized workflows.
Instead of each department maintaining separate records, every authorized user works within the same secure ecosystem.

18. Project Constraints
Initial deployment targets a single hospital.
Reliable internet is required for web access.
Users require basic training.
Compliance with local healthcare regulations is necessary.
Budget and timeline may influence feature prioritization.

19. Assumptions
The hospital will provide the required hardware and network infrastructure.
Authorized staff will use the system according to defined roles.
PostgreSQL will serve as the primary database.
Daily database backups will be configured.
The system will initially support English (with multilingual support planned later).
